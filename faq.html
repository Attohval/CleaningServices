Services

What kinds of cleaning do you offer?

Alongside domestic and commercial cleaning, you can ask about end-of-tenancy, deep, carpet and upholstery, window, after-builders, move-in/move-out and holiday-let cleaning. Tell us about your space and requirements so we can confirm whether the work, equipment and availability are suitable.

See the service options or send an enquiry.

Can I ask about a particular task or type of clean?

Yes. Include the tasks or areas you have in mind in your enquiry. Specific services are subject to discussion and confirmation before any work is agreed.

Do you bring cleaning products and equipment?

Arrangements can depend on the requirements of your space. Please mention this when you enquire so the details can be discussed and agreed in advance.

Quotes and arrangements

How do I request a quotation?

Use the contact form to share the type of cleaning, your property or premises and what you would like help with. We can discuss the requirements and quotation once we understand the scope.

What information helps you prepare a quotation?

Useful details include the size and type of space, the areas or tasks you want covered, how often you’re considering cleaning, your location and any access or scheduling considerations.

Can I arrange a one-off or regular clean?

Tell us what you’re looking for and how often you may need cleaning. Availability and arrangements will be discussed with you before anything is confirmed.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

Your details help us understand what you need. We can follow up to discuss the space, scope and practical details, then agree any suitable next steps and quotation with you.

Which areas do you cover?

Service locations are being confirmed. Share your town or postcode in the contact form and ask us to confirm availability for your area.
