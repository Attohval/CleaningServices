Draft notice
This page must be reviewed and completed by the business before the site collects personal information. It is not legal advice and does not by itself establish legal compliance.

Before launch, identify the data controller, confirm the lawful basis, set retention periods, provide verified contact details and review the form provider’s terms and privacy information. Do not enable the form until these details have been checked.

Who is responsible?

The business operating this website is responsible for deciding how enquiry information is used. Its legal name and contact details have not yet been supplied. They must be added here before launch.

What information may be collected?

If you use the quotation form, it may collect your name, email address, optional telephone number, property or premises type, town or postcode, preferred timeframe, cleaning category and message. Please do not include sensitive personal information.

Why is it used?

The information is intended to help the business understand and respond to your cleaning enquiry. The business must confirm and document the appropriate lawful basis and any further uses before enabling submissions.

Where does it go?

The enquiry form is not connected by default. Before it is enabled, the business must select and configure a form provider, review the provider’s privacy terms, identify any relevant data transfers and update this notice with the verified provider and handling details.

How long is it kept?

The business has not supplied a retention period. It must set and document an appropriate period for enquiry records and explain how deletion requests are handled before launch.

Your choices and rights

People may have rights over their personal information under applicable data protection law. The business must add a working contact method and instructions for making a request here before collecting personal information.

Cookies and website usage

This static site does not intentionally add analytics, advertising trackers or non-essential cookies. The chosen hosting provider, form provider or future integrations may affect how data is processed. Review those services and update this notice before launch. If non-essential tracking is later added, assess consent requirements before enabling it.

Changes to this notice

Update this notice when the business’s practices or service providers change. Add the effective date after the final version has been reviewed.

For questions about an enquiry, contact details for the business must be published on the contact page.
